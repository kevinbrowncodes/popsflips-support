# Pops Flips — Support

Pops Flips is an original LCD-style arcade game for Apple Watch.

## How to play

- Turn the Digital Crown to step Skip one burner left or right.
- Tap to flip what is on his burner.
- Flip when steam rises (3 points). Too early or at the last second scores 1.
- Smoke means it is about to burn. Three burns end the game.
- Misses are wiped at 200 and 500 points.
- Long-press the idle screen for best scores, sound and haptics.

## Common questions

**No sound?** Sound follows your watch's Silent Mode. Turn Silent Mode off in Control
Centre, and check Sound is on in the game's menu (long-press the idle screen).

**The game paused.** It pauses whenever you lower your wrist and carries on when you
raise it.

**Does it need an iPhone?** No. It runs on the watch by itself.
